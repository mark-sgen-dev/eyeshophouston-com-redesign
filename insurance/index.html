Medicare

West Houston Eye is a “Participating Provider” of Medicare. This means that we will complete and file your Medicare forms. Most eye exams for Medicare eligible patients are covered by Medicare. PLEASE NOTE: Glasses and contacts are not covered, with the exception of a one-time benefit following cataract surgery.

If you do not see your plan listed here, please give us a call and we would be happy to assist you. Our staff is also always available to answer any questions regarding your benefits. Please bring your insurance card and drivers license with you the day of your exam.

The cost of routine eye exams and prescription eyewear can be of real concern, especially for large families. In many cases, vision insurance can lower these annual expenses.

A vision insurance policy is not the same as medical insurance. Regular medical health insurance plans protect you against financial losses due to unexpected eye injuries or disease. Vision insurance, on the other hand, is a wellness benefit designed to provide routine eye care, prescription eyewear and other vision-related services at a reduced cost.
