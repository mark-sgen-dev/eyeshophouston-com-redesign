Jessica Wong, O.D.

Jessica Wong, O.D.

Languages: English

Home » Our Doctors » Jessica Wong, O.D.

Dr. Jessica Wong was born in Topeka, Kansas but moved to Houston, Texas as a young child. After graduating from Memorial High School, she attended the University of Texas at Austin and graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in Biology in 2005.

She attended the University of Houston – College of Optometry, graduating in 2010 as a Therapeutic Optometrist and Optometric Glaucoma Specialist.

While in school, she joined the staff of West Houston Eye in 2006, and then joined the team of doctors in 2013.

Dr. Wong is married with two daughters. She loves spending time with her children, taking them on adventures. She enjoys hiking with her husband and look forward to hiking as a family.
