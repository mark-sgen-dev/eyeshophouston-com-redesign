Comprehensive Eye Exams in Houston & So Much More

Eye Shop offers a full range of eye health services to clients of all ages, from children to older adults: routine eye exams and vision tests, eye disease diagnosis and management, immediate attention for eye emergencies, and complete eye health management. Dr. Rhodes, Dr. Warstler & Dr. Wong provide eyewear prescriptions and offer corrective laser eye surgery co-management as well. You can get in touch with our Houston eye care center to set up an appointment at your convenience and learn more about some of our key services below.

Our Eye Care Services:

Complete eye exams are not just for updating your prescription. Learn more about what your eye doctor will screen for, what to expect at your appointment, some of the advanced technology we use, and special considerations for pediatric and infant eye care.

Contact Lens Exams

A contact lens exam provides all of the measurements and testing that are required to determine if your eyes are suitable for contact lens wear. We are able to fit most patients (even hard-to-fit) and will generate your contact lens prescription.

Eye Disease Management
